Proposal 1: "The Living Page"

A storybook battle where math is magic and every answer reshapes the world.

Core idea: The battle takes place on an illustrated storybook page. A glowing territory line divides the hero's vibrant world from the enemy's corrupted darkness. Every correct answer pushes the line forward; every wrong answer lets it creep back. The math question rises from the page as a pop-up parchment flap — solving it IS the attack. Kids don't just answer questions; they cast spells by solving math.

What makes this different from what we have

  • Depth through duality: Instead of a flat mountain backdrop, the screen has two contrasting worlds — hero light vs. enemy shadow — creating instant visual drama
  • Math as the centerpiece: The pop-up flap makes the question the literal center of the screen, not a floating box competing with characters
  • Visible consequences: The territory line is a spatial metaphor kids instantly understand — "I'm winning" or "I'm losing" without reading a single number
  • Difficulty = damage: Problems rated 1-5 stars for difficulty; harder problems deal more damage regardless of answer magnitude
  • Paper hearts: HP shown as hearts (a la Zelda), not percentage bars — instantly readable for K-5
  • Character-driven attacks: Each hero has a signature weapon animation that matches their class and visual identity

The Territory Line — How It Works

Mechanics

  • Vertical glowing line starts at 50% (center)
  • Correct answer: line moves 3% toward enemy
  • Wrong answer: line moves 5% toward heroes
  • At 70%+: hero attacks deal 1.2x damage (HEAT equivalent)
  • At 30% or below: enemy attacks deal 1.15x damage
  • At 80%+: "DOMINATING!" toast with gold glow
  • Replaces the abstract momentum bar with spatial metaphor

Visual

  • Warm gold glow with animated shimmer
  • Orbs at top and bottom endpoints
  • Enemy side: purple-dark corruption overlay
  • Hero side: warm natural lighting
  • When line moves, corruption overlay width adjusts
  • Line movement animated with spring physics (0.8s, overshoot)
  • Particle trail follows line during movement
Why this replaces the momentum bar: The momentum bar is an abstract UI element that requires reading labels ("COOL", "ZONE", "HEAT"). The territory line is a spatial metaphor that a 5-year-old instantly understands: "my side is bigger = I'm winning." It creates the same mechanical effect (damage multiplier) with zero cognitive load.

★★★★★Hard, grade+12.5x base? × 7 = 63

Damage Formula

damage = (5 + hero.atk * 0.4) * difficultyMultiplier * territoryMultiplier * classMultiplier

Answer magnitude is completely removed from the formula. A kindergartner solving 2 + 3 = 5 at 3 stars deals the same relative damage as a 5th grader solving 48 ÷ 6 = 8 at 3 stars. Difficulty is about the challenge of the problem relative to the player's grade.

Attack Animations — Character-Specific

Knights (Melee, 700ms)

Each knight has a signature weapon:

  • Shadow: Dark blade — purple-black arc trail with paper-tear particles at impact point
  • Crusader: Shield charge — gold shield pushes forward, radial shockwave ring on impact
  • Paladin: Holy lance — golden light trails spear tip, cross-shaped impact flash
  • Berserker: Dual axes — two rapid crossing slashes, X-shaped cut marks
  • Great Helm: Warhammer — overhead slam, ground-crack particles radiate outward

Phases: 200ms windup (weapon raised) → 200ms strike (cross territory) → 150ms impact (particles + shake) → 150ms return

Wizards (Ranged, 800ms)

Each wizard fires a colored projectile matching their palette:

  • Stargazer: Star constellation bolt — spiral of tiny stars converge into starburst
  • Toadstool: Poison orb — green bubbling sphere arcs through air, toxic splash
  • Spellblade: Energy crescent — magic-infused blade wave fires horizontally
  • Bookworm: Page storm — torn pages swirl into a vortex aimed at enemy
  • Grand Mage: Prismatic beam — rainbow-colored beam that rotates through colors

Phases: 250ms charge (glow builds) → 200ms fire (projectile crosses) → 200ms impact (element-specific burst) → 150ms settle

Bunnies (Multi-Hit, 600ms)

Bunnies use rapid combo attacks:

  • Pepper: Rapid punches — blur-dash left-right-uppercut with speed lines between hits
  • Nova: Spark barrage — 3 crackling energy pellets fired in quick succession
  • Boulder: Ground slam — leaps up off-screen, crashes down creating shockwave
  • Blaze: Fire kicks — flaming feet leave ember trails, 3-kick combo
  • Duchess: Royal flourish — elegant spinning slash, rose petal particles

Phases: 100ms dash → 150ms per hit (2-3 hits) → 100ms return

Math Engine Upgrades

Anti-Pattern Detection

  • Track last 10 problems — if 3+ used same operand, force variety
  • If 2+ consecutive used 0 or 1 as operand, exclude them next round
  • Ensure no more than 2 consecutive same-operator problems
  • Shuffle answer button positions differently each time

Per-Operator Accuracy Tracking

  • Separate rolling accuracy for +, -, ×, ÷, fractions, etc.
  • Focus harder on weak operators — if division accuracy < 50%, weight division problems higher
  • Report per-operator mastery in victory screen

First-Strike Mechanic

  • 15% chance: enemy poses question first
  • Player correct → preemptive bonus strike
  • Player wrong → enemy attacks first
  • Bosses: always dramatic entrance, no first-strike

Paper Hearts HP System

Instead of green/red percentage bars, each hero displays 5 paper heart icons:

  • Each heart = 20% of max HP
  • Full heart: solid red ♥
  • Half heart: faded red (for 10% increments)
  • Empty heart: dark outline only
  • Hearts "tear" with a paper-rip animation when damaged
  • Hearts "fold back" when healed (origami unfold animation)

This is more readable for K-2 kids who can count hearts but can't parse "47/50 HP". Older kids (3-5) can see the exact numbers in the hero detail overlay.
